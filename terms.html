2. What the app does

Kurbl is a 2D cycling game. It reads performance data from a smart trainer, a power meter, or a cadence sensor over Bluetooth, turns it into movement along a route and, where your equipment supports it, controls the resistance.

Free permanently and without an account: connecting to your equipment and the full display of everything it reports, free riding with no time limit, holding a fixed watt or resistance value for as long as you like, the ramp test that establishes your reference power, importing your own routes from GPX files, and coins, levels, and the shop. Three of the fourteen included sessions and the first two sessions of the beginner course are free as well.

Kurbl+ unlocks, for a single payment, the features that unfold over time: the remaining included sessions and courses, writing and saving your own sessions, and your training history. Sessions you have written yourself stay rideable even if Kurbl+ is not, or is no longer, unlocked. What is included can differ between platforms. The connection layer is never put behind the purchase.

Kurbl needs no internet connection and no server of ours to run. We owe you no continuous availability of an online service, because there is no online service.

3. Health, safety, and control of your equipment

Kurbl is a game and a training tool, not a medical device. The app gives no medical, sports medicine, or therapeutic advice, makes no diagnosis, and does not monitor your health. The sessions, courses, and ramp test it includes are general suggestions based on widely used training patterns, not a plan built for you personally. Talk to a doctor before you start training, especially if you have a pre-existing condition, are pregnant, are returning after a long break from exercise, or are under 18.

While it runs, the app changes the resistance of your trainer, sometimes sharply and sometimes suddenly. Setting the equipment up safely and to the manufacturer’s instructions, judging what you can take, and deciding whether to start, continue, or stop a session are yours. The full guidance is on the Health and Safety page and forms part of these terms. Where our guidance conflicts with the safety and maintenance instructions of the manufacturer of your trainer or your bike, the manufacturer’s instructions take precedence.

4. Displayed values are guide values

Kurbl shows values it partly measures and partly calculates. Measured are the values your equipment sends. Calculated are speed, distance covered, elevation gained, and energy expenditure, among others. If you have nothing that measures watts, the app estimates your power from your cadence and from a figure you give it, or, in manual mode, works from the figure it sets for you. The app labels values like these as estimated.

Every value shown is a guide value for your training. None of them is intended for medical purposes, for competition, or for any purpose that depends on metrological accuracy. Your reference power from the ramp test is likewise an estimate following a widely used protocol, not a measurement in the technical sense.

Kurbl speaks the standard Bluetooth profiles Cycling Power Service, Fitness Machine Service, Heart Rate Service, and Cycling Speed and Cadence. We do not warrant that every individual device works fully. We do not warrant that every GPX file is usable. The app may reject files without elevation data, with corrupt points, or in a different format.
